Sillage editorial view

Who this fragrance may suit

Ripe pear and white flowers make an elegant fruity floral that is easy to wear across styles.

Good match for

  • People who enjoy ripe pear with white flowers
  • People seeking polished fruit for everyday wear or dates in spring and autumn

Consider another option if

  • People looking for a smoky, hard-edged woody scent
