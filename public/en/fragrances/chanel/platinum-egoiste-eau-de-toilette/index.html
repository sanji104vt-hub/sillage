Sillage editorial view

Who this fragrance may suit

Cool lavender moves toward warm woods. The composed aromatic profile works especially well with business and formal clothing.

Good match for

  • People who enjoy structured lavender and rosemary
  • People prioritising a clean impression for work or formal occasions

Consider another option if

  • People looking for a casual fragrance led by sweet fruit

This is Sillage's editorial interpretation of the listed composition and product attributes, not a claim made by the brand. Test on skin when possible.
