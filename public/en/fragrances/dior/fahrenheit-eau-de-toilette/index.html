Who this fragrance may suit

Violet and leather create a highly individual profile that can divide opinion but is difficult to confuse with another fragrance.

Good match for

  • People who enjoy a distinctive transition from citrus to leather
  • People seeking a substantial woody scent for autumn or winter

Consider another option if

  • People focused on soft, sweet florals
