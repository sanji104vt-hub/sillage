KITOWA

Japanese name: キトワ

JP · 9 English fragrance pages

Japan's first fragrance maison, launched in 2018 by Nippon Kodo, an incense house with 450 years of history. Every scent is built on natural oils from Japanese timber — hinoki from Mie, hiba from Aomori, kusunoki from Yakushima — layered with fine perfumery materials. The alcohol-free Eau Éclosion line uses a water base, unusual among luxury fragrances.
