Comme des Garçons

Japanese name: コム デ ギャルソン

JP · 15 English fragrance pages

The fragrance line of Comme des Garçons, the fashion house Rei Kawakubo leads from Tokyo. Its first perfume appeared in 1994. The house often breaks with convention: some works, such as Odeur 71, are built so that head and base notes cannot be separated at all.
